# 📖 How To Use

## Open the App

[▶ Open App](https://natorispd.github.io/DIN/din.html)

Just click the link — no installation needed.

> ⚠ **Use Chrome or Edge.** Safari and Firefox may not work correctly.

---

## Basic Usage (3 Steps)

### ① Tap the screen to start recording

When you open the app, a large button will appear.
Tap it to begin recording.

Speak your dream or any idea that comes to mind — just talk naturally.

---

### ② Tap again to stop

Tap the button again to stop recording. Your memo is saved automatically.

> 💡 **Tip:** You can also set the app to stop recording automatically after a period of silence (configurable in Settings).

---

### ③ Review your notes

**Swipe up** on the screen to see a list of your recorded memos.
You can copy them or save them as text files.

---

## Settings

Swipe up and open **⚙ Settings** to customize the app.

**Font size** → Increase the text size if it's too small

**Companion character** → Choose a character that speaks encouraging words during recording (free version uses browser TTS only)

**Start / End phrase** → Change what the app says when recording begins or ends

**Auto-stop on silence** → Automatically stop recording after a set number of seconds of silence — handy when your hands are busy

After changing any settings, press the **"Save Settings" button**.

---

## Saving and Deleting Memos

Tap any memo in the list to:

- **Copy** — copy the text to your clipboard
- **TXT / Markdown** — download as a text file
- **Delete** — remove the memo

---

## Troubleshooting

**Voice not recognized**
→ Make sure you're connected to the internet. Speech recognition requires an active connection.

**App won't open**
→ Try opening it in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
